KordiSure, Answered in One Paragraph

Product & Ingredients

KordiSure is a daily wellness supplement from Vanya Global made from two natural ingredients, Cordyceps and Bee Pollen. It's formulated as a one-capsule-a-day habit designed to support everyday energy, mental focus, and resilience for adults across different life stages.

KordiSure combines Cordyceps, an adaptogenic mushroom used in traditional wellness practices across Japan, China, Russia, France and the U.S., with Bee Pollen, a natural source of over 250 bioactive compounds. Together they're formulated to support steady energy, mental clarity, and general vitality rather than any single condition.

Yes. KordiSure is 100% organic with no preservatives and no additives, formulated to be a clean, natural addition to a daily routine. Full sourcing details are on the Ingredients page.

KordiSure is formulated for adults across life stages: working professionals managing long hours, women navigating hormonal and everyday wellness needs, active individuals who exercise regularly, older adults in their golden years, and couples building a shared wellness habit together.

KordiSure is formulated by Vanya Global using carefully sourced Cordyceps and Bee Pollen. For specific certification, sourcing and manufacturing details, see the Ingredients page or reach out via Contact Us.

Usage & Results

KordiSure is designed to be taken as one capsule daily, ideally alongside a morning routine such as breakfast. As with most natural daily formulations, consistency matters more than any single dose — most people build it into their routine over several weeks before assessing how it fits their day.

KordiSure is built around a one-capsule-per-day routine, so how long a bottle lasts depends on the capsule count shown on the product page at checkout. Subscribing for regular delivery is the simplest way to avoid running out.

Because Cordyceps and Bee Pollen can interact with certain medications or existing health conditions, anyone currently on medication or another supplement regimen should check with a doctor or pharmacist before adding KordiSure to their routine.

Safety

KordiSure is formulated with natural ingredients and is positioned for 0% side effects under typical use. As with any supplement, anyone who is pregnant, breastfeeding, managing a medical condition, taking medication, or has a known allergy to mushrooms or bee products should speak with a doctor before starting KordiSure.

KordiSure includes a formulation specifically framed around the "Golden Years" life stage, supporting memory, energy, immunity and restful sleep. As always, older adults managing existing conditions or medications should confirm suitability with their doctor first.
